ABES Engineering College - DOCUMENTS REQUIRED
SOURCE: https://www.abes.ac.in/courses-offered.html (Documents Required)
FETCHED: 2026-09-30
CATEGORY: admission
Bring two sets of self-attested photocopies plus originals (where stated) at final admission/reporting.
- Demand Draft of academic fee (original); demand draft of hostel fee if opted (original); uniform fees (cash
only); transport fee if opted (cash only).
- University allotment letter: 2 copies (counselling candidates). Entrance exam result: 2 copies.
- X marksheet and passing certificate; XII marksheet and passing certificate: 2 copies and original each.
Undertaking AD01 if result awaited or marksheets/degree not available.
- Diploma/graduation marksheets (all years) and PDC/Degree: 2 copies and original each.
- Transfer Certificate (UP Board / UP Technical Board): 1 copy and original. Migration certificate (other
boards/universities): 1 copy and original.
- Character certificate: 1 copy and original.
- Caste certificate (OBC certificate issued after 01.04.2025): 2 copies, only for SC/ST/OBC.
- Income certificate (issued after 01.04.2025) or current-year ITR, if interested in any government
scholarship: 2 copies. EWS income certificate: 2 copies (format:
https://www.abes.ac.in/pdf/admissions/EWS Certificate.pdf).
- Gap affidavit on Rs 10 stamp paper, if any: 1 copy and original.
- Domicile certificate: 2 copies, needed if qualifying exam was passed outside UP but parents reside in UP.
- Medical fitness certificate from CMO/registered doctor in prescribed format: 1 copy
(https://www.abes.ac.in/pdf/medical certificate.pdf). Blood group test report: 1 copy.
- Photos: 3 of candidate and 1 each of father and mother; write student name and branch on the back.
- Aadhar card of self, father and mother: 2 copies.
- Declaration/Undertaking RO-S21 (original) and Internet Use Undertaking CA-S1 (original).
Note: keep one set of photocopies for personal/scholarship use. Originals are returned only after
University verification.
