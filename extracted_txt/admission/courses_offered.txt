ELIGIBILITY (as per UPTAC brochure 2026-27, subject to AKTU changes)
- B.Tech 1st year (4 yrs): passed 10+2 with Physics/Mathematics/Chemistry/Computer
Science/Electronics/IT/Biology/Informatics Practices/Biotechnology/Technical
Vocational/Agriculture/Engineering Graphics/Business Studies/Entrepreneurship per table 1.3(a)
(Annexure-1: https://abes.ac.in/pdf/Annexure-1.pdf); at least 45% (40% reserved category) in those
subjects together. OR passed D.Voc. in same/allied sector. Bridge courses are offered to students from
diverse backgrounds.
- B.Tech Lateral Entry (2nd year, 3 yrs): diploma (3 yrs / 2 yrs lateral) with at least 45% (40% SC/ST) in
any engineering branch except Agriculture Engg., Pharmacy, Architecture, from a BTEUP-recognized
institution or equivalent; OR B.Sc. from a UGC-recognized university with at least 45% (40% SC/ST) and
10+2 with Mathematics (allotted if seats remain after diploma students); OR D.Voc.
- BCA (3 yrs): 10+2 in any stream.
- M.Tech (2 yrs): bachelor's degree or equivalent with at least 50% (45% reserved category).
- MCA (2 yrs): BCA / B.Sc. (CS) / B.Sc. (IT) / B.E. or B.Tech (CSE/IT) or equivalent; OR any graduation
with Mathematics at 10+2 or graduation level, at least 50% (45% SC/ST).
COUNSELLING PRIORITY (AICTE Approval Process Handbook 2026-27)
- B.Tech 1st yr: JEE Main (current year) candidates first, then merit in qualifying exam; or as per UP
Govt./University directive.
