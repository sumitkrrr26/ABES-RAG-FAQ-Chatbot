OVERVIEW
The Department of CSE with Specialization in AI and ML was established in 2020. It offers a 4-year
full-time B.Tech CSE-AIML programme with a total intake of 360. Facilities include a Project Lab and
Advanced AI & ML Labs. The department runs in-house training, industrial training, workshops, expert
talks, guest lectures and alumni interactions, with emphasis on live projects, internships, hackathons,
certifications and competitive programming.
Highlights: ranked 1 in AKTU (per AKTU first-year results) among colleges offering B.Tech CSE with AI &
ML specialization (as stated by ABES); industry-academia connect; advanced training and research
centres; regular industrial talks.
Placement in 2025-26 named Amazon, Accenture, Capgemini India, Cognizant, HCL Technologies,
Infosys, Josh Technology Group, Veersa Technologies, Pibit.AI, MAC Softwares, Infra Market.
Career paths: Machine Learning Engineer, AI Engineer, ML Architect, AI scientist in R&D.
VISION: to become a globally recognized, student-centric department fostering high-quality learning and
future-ready professionals in AI and ML through transformative education, applied research, innovation
and industry-integrated practices.
MISSION: student-centric outcome-based AI/ML education; ethical, socially responsible lifelong learners;
collaborative environment with global academic and industry collaborations; culture of innovation, research
and entrepreneurship.
PEOs: (1) computing, analytics and engineering fundamentals; (2) research-oriented thinking and
innovation in AI/ML; (3) entrepreneurship and project management for AI-based solutions; (4) continuous
learning through professional certification; (5) ethics, data privacy, security and fairness in intelligent
systems.
PSOs: (1) apply mathematical foundations, algorithms and CS principles to model and design advanced
systems; (2) solve complex computation problems with AI/ML in analytics, software, network and
intelligent systems; (3) apply ethical AI solutions for societal benefit and sustainable development.
Programme Outcomes PO1-PO11 follow the standard NBA/AICTE list (engineering knowledge, problem
analysis, design, investigations, tool usage, engineer and the world, ethics, teamwork, communication,
project management and finance, life-long learning).
HOD: Dr. Deepali Dev, Associate Professor & HOD (Ph.D). Her message: department focuses on AI, ML
and Data Science; students take part in Smart India Hackathon, ACM ICPC and Google coding contests.
